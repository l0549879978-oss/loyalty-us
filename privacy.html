Privacy Policy

Draft for legal review · Last updated: September 18, 2026

Legal review required: This policy is a practical launch draft, not legal advice. The operator's legal name, mailing address, privacy contact, retention periods, and any state-specific notices must be completed and approved by qualified U.S. counsel before paid commercial use.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how LOYALTY US collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when businesses and their customers use the LOYALTY US website, dashboards, QR enrollment pages, loyalty wallets, and related services.

2. Information we collect

For business accounts, we may collect the business name, contact name, email address, telephone number, login credentials, subscription and billing status, and account activity. For customer loyalty accounts, we may collect first and last name, telephone number, email address, address, city, state, ZIP code, birth date, login credentials, participating-business memberships, purchase amounts, reward balances, redemption history, and related loyalty activity.

We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, pages viewed, error logs, security events, and analytics needed to operate and protect the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; operate QR enrollment, purchases, rewards, redemptions, wallets, and dashboards; process subscriptions; provide support; communicate service notices; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain security; diagnose errors; comply with law; and improve the service.

4. Businesses and customer information

Participating businesses determine their loyalty offers and may access information about customers enrolled with their business. Each business is independently responsible for providing any required notices, obtaining required consent, using customer information lawfully, honoring privacy rights, and limiting staff access. Businesses may not use LOYALTY US to upload or process information they are not authorized to use.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We may disclose information to vendors that help provide hosting, databases, payment processing, analytics, communications, security, and customer support. These currently may include Vercel for hosting, Neon for database services, Stripe for payment services, and configured analytics providers. Payment-card information is handled by the payment processor and should not be stored directly by LOYALTY US.

We may also disclose information when required by law; to protect users, rights, safety, or the service; to investigate fraud or abuse; or in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of the service, subject to applicable law.

6. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

The service may use cookies, browser local storage, and similar technologies to keep users signed in, remember preferences, support installable web-app features, measure usage, and protect accounts. Browser settings can limit these technologies, but doing so may prevent parts of the service from working correctly.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Specific retention periods must be finalized before commercial launch. Information may remain for a limited time in backups after deletion from active systems.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

9. Privacy choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or information about disclosures, and to appeal a denied request. Identity verification may be required. Some information may be retained where permitted or required by law. A working privacy contact method must be published here before commercial launch.

10. Children's privacy

LOYALTY US is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Businesses must not enroll a child or submit a child's information without all notices, consent, and other protections required by applicable law.

11. U.S. service and international users

The service is intended for use in the United States. Information may be processed and stored in the United States or other locations used by our service providers. Users outside the United States should obtain legal review before using the service.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required by law.

13. Contact

The operator's legal name, mailing address, and dedicated privacy email must be inserted here before paid commercial use.
